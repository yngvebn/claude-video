# Style: field journal

*(story-video original · no built-in look yet: author as a custom look, closest built-in: whiteboard)*

A researcher's field notebook laid open on a desk: ink sketches drawn on as they're described, taped-in photos and
tickets, margin notes, measurements, arrows and small watercolour washes. It's curious, observant and personal, a fit
for travel, research, "what we learned" retrospectives and user-interview stories.

## Signature
| Item | On screen |
|---|---|
| Page | A two-page spread of cream, lightly grid-ruled paper with a gutter shadow, the page edges visible, the desk wood grain at the margins |
| Ink | Fine black and sepia pen lines, drawn stroke by stroke with a slight wobble; cross-hatching for shade |
| Washes | Loose watercolour fills bleeding past the lines in two or three muted colours |
| Clippings | Taped-in items (a photo drawn as a polaroid, a receipt, a ticket stub) with translucent tape corners and slight tilt |
| Notes | Handwritten margin notes, circled question marks, underlines, "!!", dated entries in the top corner |
| Measure | Specimen-style labels with leader lines, and scale bars |
| Camera | Top-down; slow pans between entries across the spread, and a page turn between chapters |
| Palette | Cream, sepia, black ink, sage, rust, dusty blue |

## Direction
Every chapter is a dated journal entry. Sketch the subject first in ink as the narrator describes it, then add washes,
then label it with leader lines. Tape a clipping in when a concrete artefact is mentioned. The margins carry the
narrator's asides as handwritten notes. Keep it intimate and unhurried, with plenty of empty paper. The page turn is a
real curl with the next spread revealed underneath. Sound is pen scratching, paper and a quiet acoustic guitar.

## Narration
Sketch strokes follow the description, labels land on the named parts, and clippings tape in on "we found" or "here's
the receipt". Margin notes carry the funny asides. No caption bar.

## Block ideas for a look
`entry { date, title, at }`, `sketch { shape | path, at }`, `wash { area, color, at }`, `clip { kind, x, y, at }`,
`label { target, text, at }`, `note { txt, x, y, at }`, `turn { at }`.
